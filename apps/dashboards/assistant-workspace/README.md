# Assistant Workspace

Use this workspace for assistant-facing workflows and operational drafts.

## Scope

- Customer-support answer drafts.
- Product Q&A workflows.
- Content task queues and prompt drafts.
- Owner-dashboard summaries prepared by an assistant.
- Loyalty notification message drafts for browser push delivery.

## Browser Notification Notes

Loyalty notifications should be drafted here as in-app/browser push messages for customers who explicitly opt in through Chrome, Safari, or another browser with Web Push support. Do not plan loyalty delivery around email, SMS, WhatsApp, or paid messaging services unless the owner later asks for those channels.

## Boundaries

- Do not store private customer lists or browser push subscription exports here.
- Keep engineering implementation details in `developer-workspace/`.
- Keep business approvals and campaign decisions in `owner-workspace/`.
